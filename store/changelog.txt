Version 1.4 - What's new

- Supports the fenix 9 family. Carb Burn now builds for fenix 9 (43/47 mm), fenix 9 Pro (43/47/51 mm) and fenix 9 Pro Solar (47/51 mm), alongside the Edge and fenix models it already supported. Note that no fenix 9 has ridden this field yet - it is known to build for them, not yet proven on one.

- The first minute of a ride no longer records an inflated carb/fat rate. The field rescales its power model to your watch's calorie total; at the first pedalling sample there is almost no model energy to divide by, so if your watch had already counted calories - a power meter that wakes late, a timer started before you roll out, a light at the start - the rate written to your FIT file could be several times too high. That rescaling is now bounded.

- A flaky power meter no longer under-reads your carb rate. A missing reading is now told apart from a measured 0 W and modelled at your last known power for up to 2.5 seconds. Note this means a ride through a dropping-out meter now records MORE carbohydrate than the same ride did on 1.3 - the old version silently discarded that work. Rides without dropouts are unchanged.

- The carb % reads "--" instead of a number when there is almost no substrate being burned, and the colour greys out with it, rather than showing a percentage of nearly nothing.
