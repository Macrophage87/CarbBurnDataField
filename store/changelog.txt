Version 1.4

- Supports the fenix 9 family: fenix 9 (43/47 mm), fenix 9 Pro (43/47/51 mm) and fenix 9 Pro Solar (47/51 mm). The field builds for these watches but has not yet been ridden on one.
- The first minute of a ride no longer records an inflated carb/fat rate. Carb Burn scales its power model to your watch's calorie total, and at the very first pedal stroke there was almost no model energy to divide by - so if your watch had already counted calories (a late-waking power meter, a timer started before you roll out, a light at the start), the rate written to your FIT file could be several times too high. That scaling is now bounded.
- A dropping-out power meter no longer under-reads your carb rate. A missing reading is now told apart from a real 0 W and carried at your last known power for up to 2.5 seconds. Note this means a ride through a flaky meter records MORE carbohydrate than the same ride did on 1.3 - the old version silently threw that work away. Rides without dropouts are unchanged.
- The carb % reads "--" instead of a number when almost no fuel is burning, and the colour greys out with it.

Version 1.3

- Red zone now starts at your FTP. Red previously began near the 90%-carb power, which for most riders sits above FTP, so it rarely appeared. Red now flags efforts above your one-hour power.

Version 1.2

- The colour no longer lags the numbers. The rolling readouts and their zone colour now come from the same values.
- The blue fat-max band is defined by grams per hour rather than watts, so it marks where you are actually burning the most fat.
- Better readability on light backgrounds.
- The per-second FIT fields now record rates in grams per hour, not running totals. Cumulative grams are recorded as ride totals.
- Larger grid text on big screens such as the Edge 1050.

Version 1.0

- Real-time carbohydrate and fat burn estimated from power, anchored to your FTP and LT1.
- Layouts that adapt to the field shape, including a full-screen grid showing rolling, lap and ride-average carb g/h, fat g/h and carb %.
- Carbs spent and a glycogen-remaining gauge from your body weight.
- Fuelling-equilibrium power: the wattage at which your carb burn matches what you are eating.
- Carb and fat recorded into the FIT file for post-ride review.
- Settings for FTP, LT1, gross efficiency, body weight and carb intake.
